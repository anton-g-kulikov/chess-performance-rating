# 2022 performance race

Games from events that ended in **2022** (the full year), read from the January 2022 – April 2023 FIDE lists. Everyone starts again on January 1. Player pool: the 214 players in FIDE's open top 100 for classical, rapid or blitz on the January, April, July or October 2022 list, or the January 2023 list.

**TPR** is the FIDE performance rating over all rated games in the window (average opponent + dp from the FIDE table). **True** is the rating whose expected score against the actual opponents equals the actual score. **FIDE** is the player's rating on the January 2022 list; **Δ** is TPR minus it.

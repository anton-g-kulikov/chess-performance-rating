TPR: Tournament Performance Rating

TPR is the rating a player would need to make the same score against the same opponents. FIDE works it out in two steps.

  1. Take the average rating of the opponents.
  2. Add the value from FIDE's conversion table for the percentage score. Scores below 50% subtract the same amounts.

Average rating of the opponents. FIDE's 400-point rule applies: an opponent rated more than 400 below the player is counted as exactly 400 below.

True
A stricter version of TPR, worked out against each opponent's rating instead of their average. The two differ most when the opponents' ratings vary widely. A 0% or 100% score has no True value.

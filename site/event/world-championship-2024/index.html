How it works

This page

Same opponents

Every player both of them met in rated games of the chosen format during the 12 months, with each one's results against that player in the order they were played. The rating beside a name is the opponent's average rating across those games.

Expected and played

FIDE's expected score for one game depends only on the difference between two ratings. It is applied twice, to the two FIDE ratings and to the two 12-month classical TPRs, and multiplied by the 14 games of the match. It says what each gap is worth, and is not a prediction: it knows nothing about preparation, colours or match play.

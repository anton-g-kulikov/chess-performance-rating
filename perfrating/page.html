TPR: Tournament Performance Rating

TPR is the rating a player's results were worth: the rating someone would need to score exactly what they scored against the same opponents. It is calculated the way FIDE does it, in two steps.

  1. Take the average rating of the opponents.
  2. Add the value from FIDE's conversion table for the percentage score. Scores below 50% subtract the same amounts.

The other columns

True
The rating at which the expected score against each actual opponent adds up to the actual score. It is the more precise measure when opponents' ratings are spread out, and it doesn't exist for 0% or 100%.
Score
Points from rated games, with the percentage. Win 1, draw ½, loss 0.
Avg opp
Average rating of the opponents faced. As in FIDE's own calculations, an opponent rated more than 400 points below the player counts as the player's rating minus 400.
FIDE
The player's current FIDE rating in that format.
Δ
TPR minus that rating. Green means playing above the FIDE rating, red below it.

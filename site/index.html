An alternative to the FIDE rating, which carries results forward for years. Here players are ranked only by their performance over the last twelve FIDE rating periods, in classical, rapid and blitz. No recent games, no ranking.

How it's calculated

TPR is FIDE's performance rating over every rated game in the window: the average opponent rating plus the FIDE table value (dp) for the percentage score. All of a player's events in a format are pooled into one result.

True is the rating at which the expected score against those exact opponents equals the actual score. It allows for how spread out the opponents' ratings were.

Δ is TPR minus the player's current FIDE rating in that format. Opponents rated more than 400 points below the player count as the player's rating minus 400, as in FIDE's own calculations.

Source: game-by-game rating calculations on ratings.fide.com. Code: chess-performance-rating.
